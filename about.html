The Problem

Running a business on guesswork is expensive.

Every retailer, brand, and product business faces the same three headaches — and every one of them costs real money.

📦

Stockouts

You under-order your best seller, run out mid-season, and watch customers buy it from someone else instead.

🧊

Overstock

You over-order to "be safe," and end up with cash tied up in a warehouse full of slow-moving inventory.

😵

Spreadsheet Guesswork

Planning next quarter means eyeballing last year's numbers and hoping nothing's changed. It usually has.

The Solution

A forecast built from your own sales — not a generic guess.

ForecastIQ studies the sales patterns unique to your business — seasonality, trends, product mix, growth — and turns them into a forward-looking forecast you can actually plan around.

01

Upload your sales history

Export the sales data you already track — daily, weekly, or monthly, for one product or your whole catalog — and upload it. No special formatting, no IT team required.

02

ForecastIQ learns your business

It automatically finds the patterns behind your numbers — busy seasons, slow months, growth trends, product-by-product differences — and builds a forecasting model tuned specifically to you.

03

Get a clear forecast, whenever you need one

Ask for the next 3, 6, or 12 months and get a straightforward answer: how much you're likely to sell, product by product, store by store. Download it, share it, plan around it.

04

Track accuracy and revisit anytime

Every forecast is saved with an easy-to-read report — so you can look back at what was predicted, compare it to what actually happened, and keep improving how you plan.
